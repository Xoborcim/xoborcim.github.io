Common questions

How do I place a cat?
Tap a cell once to mark it ✕, and tap it again quickly (a double-tap) to place a cat. Tapping an ✕ later removes it.

How do I reach cells in the middle of the cube?
Pick a floor under the cube to look straight down on one layer. The floors above it disappear. The arrow button puts the view back if you get lost.

What are the cube buttons?
They move you through the fourth dimension. Each one is a whole 3D cube, and a cat icon shows which cubes already have their cat.

Why are some cells red?
With assist on, red cells touch a cat in a neighboring cube, so they can't hold a cat. Faded cells are ruled out by cats you've already placed.

I'm stuck. Is this puzzle impossible?
Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic alone. Try a hint, or read the strategy guide. If you still think a puzzle is wrong, email us the level number.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
In regions that require it, an "Ad privacy choices" link appears at the bottom of the home screen.

Is my progress backed up?
Your level, stars and daily streak are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
