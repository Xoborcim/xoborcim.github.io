Support

Lost in the hills? Write and we'll help you find your way.

Contact

Email xoborcim.games@gmail.com. If it's about a particular map, mention the date or difficulty, and your device (iPhone model and iOS version, or browser). We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

Is the daily map the same on the web and on iPhone?
Yes. Everyone gets the same map each day, wherever they play.

I'm stuck. Is there a guess I have to make?
Never. Every map can be solved by reasoning alone. Try a hint: it points to a square you can work out next and explains why.

I bought the Cartographer's Edition on another device.
Open the Cartographer's Edition page in the app and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I change my ad consent?
In the EEA, UK and other regions that require it, open Settings › Advertising › Ad privacy choices in the free version.

Where are the ads?
The free iPhone version shows an occasional ad after a free-play map, never during a solve, and never on the daily map. The Cartographer's Edition removes them. The web version has no ads.

Is my progress backed up?
Progress is kept on your device (or in your browser on the web). Deleting the app or clearing your browser's site data erases it.
