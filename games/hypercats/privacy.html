Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information the Hypercats 4D app ("the app", "we") uses, why, and the choices you have. Questions: xoborcim.games@gmail.com.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for your name, email address or account details, and we don't run servers that collect information about you. Your level, stars, daily streak and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: an occasional ad between levels, and optional videos you can choose to watch for an extra life or hint. The app only requests non-personalized ads and never asks to track you, so your device's advertising identifier is not used for tracking.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may still process information such as your IP address, device model and operating system, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads.

In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and other regions that require it, the app shows Google's consent message before any ads are requested. You can change your choices any time from the "Ad privacy choices" link on the app's home screen. How Google uses information from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Since we don't hold information about you ourselves, the quickest way is through the controls above, Google's My Ad Center, or by contacting us.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows when it last changed.
