# Research data

Generated by `npm run research` (600 candidates per row, deterministic seeds). Baseline: 7×7 board, budget 2, 2 generations, 6 editable cells, 3 locked flames, 1 lantern, protect and clue objectives allowed.

Outcome columns are shares of all candidates. "Unique" candidates are those for which objectives could be added until exactly one placement remained (everything from "too few possibilities" rightwards). An outcome is the first failing check: a "trivial" puzzle is unique but solved by one round of direct neighbour counting; "lookahead" needs the bounded local-search rule (L4); "needs branching" needs a one-step hypothesis (L5); "needs guessing" leaves the analyzer stuck.

### Q7: do accepted puzzles settle into simple patterns?

Of 2224 accepted puzzles across the experiments above, the intended configuration is a still life at the final generation in 31.5% and a period-2 oscillator (e.g. blinker) in 8.2%. The rest are still changing when the puzzle ends.

### Trivial and stuck candidates

- Trivial: 164 candidates. 95.1% were settled by a single neighbour-count step; 45.1% had T = 1.
- Stuck (analyzer cannot finish even with one-step hypotheses): 36 of 2669 unique candidates. Mean T = 2.00, mean undecided cells when it stopped = 8.00, mean placements = 61.89.
- Branching (solved only with a one-step hypothesis): 137 of 2669. Mean T = 2.02, mean placements = 56.09.

### Q1: how much does one lantern constrain the start?

Over all candidates that reached lantern selection (baseline, 22 possible placements):

- T = 1: one lantern alone is satisfied by a median of 6 placements (mean 8.24); it is already unique in 9.2% of 468 boards.
- T = 2: one lantern alone is satisfied by a median of 3 placements (mean 4.65); it is already unique in 20.6% of 383 boards.
- T = 3: one lantern alone is satisfied by a median of 3 placements (mean 4.11); it is already unique in 24.8% of 452 boards.

### Q4: the speed-of-light limit

Life patterns grow at most one cell per generation, so a lantern lit at generation T is at most T cells (Chebyshev) from some cell burning at the start; it can be farther from the editable region only by relaying through locked flames. Among 1376 unique candidates in the T and distance experiments, 9.0% have a lantern at exactly distance T.
