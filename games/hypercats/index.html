Hypercats 4D

One cat per color. Four dimensions. No touching. A logic puzzle you can spin in your hand, for iPhone.

App Store · Coming soon Strategy guide

How it plays

  • One cat per region. Every colored region of the hypercube gets exactly one cat.
  • No sharing a slab. No two cats share a cube, a floor, a row or a column.
  • No touching. Cats can't be neighbors, not even diagonally, and that includes the cubes on either side.
  • Tap and double-tap. Tap a cell to mark it ✕, double-tap to place a cat. A wrong cat costs one of three hearts.

Getting around four dimensions

  • Spin the cube. Drag to turn it like a turntable, tilt it up and down, and pinch to zoom.
  • Step through the fourth dimension. Each cube along the fourth direction is a whole 3D board of its own.
  • Look down on a floor. Pick a floor to see one layer from above, like a flat puzzle.
  • Assist. Cells your cats rule out fade away, and cells that touch a cat in the next cube over glow red.

Ways to play

  • Endless levels. The same puzzles for everyone, in blocks of ten that start gently and slowly mix in harder puzzles, each ending on a boss. The first five are warm-ups with free hints.
  • Daily puzzle. One shared hypercube a day, easy on Monday and expert by Sunday. Keep your streak going.
  • Stars. Solve without mistakes or hints for three stars.
  • Hints that teach. Learn the techniques as you go, from "last cell standing" to "what if?". The strategy guide covers them all.

Free to play with ads. Optional videos give an extra life or an extra hint.
