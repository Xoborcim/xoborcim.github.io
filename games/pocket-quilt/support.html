Common questions

How do I sew a patch?
Join a new piece to blocks already on the board so they make a 2×2 square of one color. The patch is sewn away for points and adds a square to your quilt.

What is Pocket Dye?
Drag a piece into POCKET, then spend a full spool of thread to dye it another color. Clearing lines and sewing patches refills your thread.

I bought Remove Ads or bombs on another device.
Open the Shop or Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
In regions that require it, open Settings in the game and tap Ad privacy options. You can also change tracking permission in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Is my progress backed up?
Your progress and quilts are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
