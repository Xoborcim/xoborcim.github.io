Pocket Quilt

Sew patches. Dye fabric. Make a quilt. A little patchwork puzzler for iPhone.

App Store · Coming soon

How it plays

  • Place fabric pieces. Drag pieces onto the board. Fill a row or column to clear it.
  • Sew patches. Join a piece to matching fabric already on the board to make a 2×2 square of one color, and it's sewn away.
  • Dye your pocket. Keep a piece in your pocket for later, and spend a spool of thread to dye it the color you need.
  • Twist and blast. Rotate one piece per set, and keep a bomb handy for tight spots.

Ways to play

  • Classic. Play for as long as you can keep the board clear.
  • Daily Puzzle. The same puzzle for everyone each day. Earn stars and share a spoiler-free result.
  • Time Trial. Two minutes. As many points as you can sew.
  • The Quilt Room. Six quilts to make, from sawtooth stars to log cabin, square by square.

Free to play with ads. An optional purchase removes ads between games.
