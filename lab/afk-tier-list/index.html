How the overall score works

  • Overall rank uses Competitive Investment where listed, otherwise Optimal Investment.
  • Each mode is weighted: Arena 1.4, Dream Realm 1.3, Campaign 1.0, Forked Fate 1.0, Faction Tower 0.3, Guild Hunt 0.2, Temporal Rift 0.2, AbEx 0.1.
  • Faction Tower for Dimensional, Celestial and Hypogean heroes uses the King's Tower.
  • AbEx ranks are mostly theoretical, since server-based competition is hard to test.
  • Meta heroes with six or more S ranks and one weak mode get adjusted (for example Merlin, Shalltear, Framton).
  • The overall rank can mislead. For a specific mode, rank by that mode above: some strong picks there have a low overall score.

Investment notation

  • A5* = Ascended with 5 stars. T = Transcendent. M = Mythic, L = Legendary.
  • SI 0 = item unlocked; SI 20 and SI 30 are later breakpoints.
  • < between two breakpoints means both are good, the right one is better.
  • X = not enough data yet.
